# Student data notice: Draftwork (template)

*Adapt the bracketed parts for your institution and have them reviewed by your privacy or records office.*

**Purpose.** [Course/Department] uses Draftwork for some written assignments. It records how a piece of writing was produced so that your instructor can talk with you about your process. It does not grade your work and it does not decide whether you wrote it.

**Data collected.** The text you write and a log of your edits with their times; when the writing window gains or loses focus; the length (not content) of refused pastes; and what you enter yourself: your name and student number, optional process notes, an optional AI-use disclosure, and the file names and fingerprints of any outlines you attach. Nothing outside the writing page is recorded.

**Data sent during writing.** About once a minute, a SHA-256 fingerprint of your progress is sent via [relay operator] to a public RFC 3161 timestamp service ([FreeTSA / DigiCert / Sectigo]). A fingerprint cannot be used to recover your text.

**Where your work is stored.** In your browser until you submit. The file you upload is stored in [LMS name] with your other coursework.

**Who has access.** Your instructor and [teaching assistants / academic integrity office, if applicable]. You can see the same view before submitting.

**How it is used.** A process pattern can only lead to a conversation, never directly to a penalty. [Describe any further process, e.g. referral rules.]

**Retention.** Submitted files are kept for [period] under [policy]. Instructor check-in notes are kept [where/how long].

**Legal basis / rights.** [FERPA: education records / GDPR: lawful basis, e.g. public task]. You may request access to or correction of your records by contacting [office/email].

**Alternatives.** If you cannot work online at a computer for an assignment, contact your instructor, who will arrange an alternative.
