Draftwork

Records how a take-home piece of writing was produced, so an instructor can replay it and ask about it. It does not try to prove authorship; it makes faking authorship cost more than doing the work.

I’m writing

Open the assignment link from your instructor, write in the sandbox, then save the .draftwork file and upload it to your LMS. You can preview exactly what your instructor will see.

I’m teaching

Create an assignment link, then open your class’s .draftwork files to replay them: integrity checks, flags with their benign readings, process signals, and 3–5 questions for a five-minute check-in.
Process, not productThe full edit history must regenerate the text exactly.
No institutional backendThe file lives on the student’s computer; only checkpoint hashes are sent, for timestamping.
Flags, never scoresEach flag stands alone with its benign readings beside it.
The conversation is the enforcementThe viewer surfaces specific moments to ask about.
The student sees what the instructor seesFlags included, before submitting.
