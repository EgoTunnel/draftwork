Writing your assignment in Draftwork

Draftwork is a word processor that records how your writing comes together. You write in it like in Word, then upload one file to your course.

Start

  1. Open the link your instructor posted in your course site. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, in a normal window: a private (Incognito or InPrivate) window deletes your draft when you close it.
  2. Type your name and student number. Check them carefully: they can’t be changed later.
  3. Read what’s recorded, tick the box, and click Start.

Write

  • Write as you normally would. Your draft is kept on that computer as you go; you can close the page and come back.
  • Start online. If the connection drops while you write, keep writing: your work is safe.
  • If pasting from other places is turned off, put quotes in the Sources panel on the right, then copy them into your writing from there.

When you’re finished: save, then upload

  1. Click Save file to upload… (top right). A .draftwork file is saved, usually to your Downloads folder.
  2. Upload that .draftwork file to the assignment’s dropbox in your course, the same way you’d upload a Word document.
  3. Keep the receipt code Draftwork shows you.

Saving doesn’t submit your assignment. It isn’t submitted until you upload the file. Upload the .draftwork file, not a Word document.

Good to know

  • Another computer? Save your file, then on the other computer open the same link and choose Continue from a file I saved…
  • Can’t find the saved file? Press Ctrl+J in Chrome or Edge to see your downloads.
  • Before you upload, click Preview to check your word count and anything your instructor might ask about.
  • Something not working? Save your file and contact your instructor.
