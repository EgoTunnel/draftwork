Open your assignment

Draftwork is tested in Chrome and Edge. It may work in this browser, but if you can, use Chrome or Edge for this assignment. If anything goes wrong, save your file often and tell your instructor.

Your edits, and when you made them, are kept with your work so your instructor can replay how it was written. More about privacy

    Started on another computer?

    Choose the .draftwork file you saved last time (usually in Downloads), and you’ll carry on where you left off.

    Continue your draft

    Your draft is kept in this browser only. Clearing browsing data, a private window, or another browser or computer won’t have it. Save your file often: it’s your backup, and it’s what you upload to your course.

    Not ? On a shared computer, start your own draft. Theirs is kept under Earlier versions.

    Start writing

    Shown to your instructor with your work. Check it carefully: it can’t be changed later.
    Before you start
    • Every edit you make, and when you make it, is saved in your file so your instructor can replay how you wrote.
    • While you write, Draftwork sends a short code made from your progress (not your text) to an online clock service, so the time is on record. Your text stays on this computer until you upload the file.
    • Your instructor may ask you about moments in your writing. Nothing here is a grade: it can only lead to a conversation.
    • Use a normal browser window, not a private one (Incognito or InPrivate). Your draft is kept in this browser, and closing a private window deletes it.
