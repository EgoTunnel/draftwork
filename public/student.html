    What Draftwork records

    Every edit you make (typing, deleting, moving text), when it happened, and when the window loses focus. Pasting from outside the document is handled by the assignment’s policy; refused pastes are logged by length only, never their content.

    Every minute or so the sandbox sends a hash of your progress to a timestamp service. Only that hash leaves your computer; your text stays here until you save the file and upload it to your LMS yourself.

    You can open the exact view your instructor will see at any time with Preview. A process pattern can only ever lead to a conversation, never directly to a penalty.
